# My Map 


## Level 1: Absolute Beginner

- **Core Concepts:**
    - [[Basic Guitar Strings|Learn the names of guitar strings.]]     
    - [[Guitar Anatomy|Understand guitar anatomy.]]
    - Grasp fundamental techniques (holding the pick, posture, etc.).
- **Exercises:**
    - Practice tuning the guitar.
    - Focus on playing simple single notes.
- **Outcome:** Familiarity with the instrument.
---
## Level 2: Basic Mechanics
- **Core Concepts:**
    - Develop strumming mechanics.
    - Learn basic open chords (e.g., G, C, D, Em, Am).
    - Play simple chord transitions.
- **Exercises:**
    - Practice downstrokes and upstrokes.
    - Work on simple 4-chord progressions.
- **Outcome:** Ability to play basic songs with open chords.

---
## Level 3: Rhythmic Mastery
- **Core Concepts:**
    - Learn basic strumming patterns.
    - Introduce timing and rhythm.
    - Play along with a metronome.
- **Exercises:**
    - Use simple songs to master rhythm.
    - Practice quarter notes, eighth notes, and rests.
- **Outcome:** Develop consistent rhythm and timing.

---

## Level 4: Expanding Chord Vocabulary

- **Core Concepts:**
    - Introduce barre chords (e.g., F major).
    - Learn power chords and how to move them.
    - Understand the capo and its uses.
- **Exercises:**
    - Play songs that combine open and barre chords.
    - Practice transitioning between power chords.
- **Outcome:** Increased flexibility in playing diverse songs.


---

## Level 5: Fingerpicking Techniques

- **Core Concepts:**
    - Learn basic fingerpicking patterns.
    - Introduce Travis picking.
- **Exercises:**
    - Practice alternating thumb patterns.
    - Combine fingerpicking with chord progressions.
- **Outcome:** Ability to play fingerstyle songs.
    

---

## Level 6: Intermediate Lead Guitar

- **Core Concepts:**
    - Learn the pentatonic scale (minor and major).
    - Understand hammer-ons, pull-offs, and slides.
- **Exercises:**
    - Practice scale patterns across the fretboard.
	    - Work on simple solos or riffs.
- **Outcome:** Basic lead guitar proficiency.
    

---

## Level 7: Advanced Rhythm Guitar

- **Core Concepts:**
    
    - Introduce chord embellishments (sus2, sus4, add9).
        
    - Learn syncopation and off-beat strumming.
        
- **Exercises:**
    
    - Play songs with complex rhythm structures.
        
    - Experiment with muting and dynamics.
        
- **Outcome:** Enhanced rhythm playing for varied styles.
    

---

## Level 8: Advanced Lead Guitar

- **Core Concepts:**
    
    - Learn the CAGED system.
        
    - Understand basic modes (e.g., Dorian, Phrygian).
        
- **Exercises:**
    
    - Map the fretboard using the CAGED system.
        
    - Improvise using modes over backing tracks.
        
- **Outcome:** Mastery of lead guitar across styles.
    

---

## Level 9: Music Theory for Guitarists

- **Core Concepts:**
    
    - Learn chord construction (major, minor, diminished, etc.).
        
    - Understand key signatures and scales.
        
    - Apply intervals and harmonization.
        
- **Exercises:**
    
    - Build chords from scales.
        
    - Analyze songs for theory application.
        
- **Outcome:** Deeper musical understanding and creativity.
    

---

## Level 10: Creating Music

- **Core Concepts:**
    
    - Develop songwriting skills.
        
    - Learn basic recording techniques.
        
    - Understand arrangement and layering.
        
- **Exercises:**
    
    - Compose original riffs, melodies, and chord progressions.
        
    - Experiment with multi-track recording.
        
- **Outcome:** Ability to create and record original music.
    
    

---

## Resources for Each Level

- **YouTube Channels:** Justin Guitar, Marty Music. etc
- **Books:** "Hal Leonard Guitar Method," "The Guitar Handbook."    

---

With dedication and structured practice, this roadmap will guide you from a beginner to a skilled guitarist capable of creating and performing music confidently.
