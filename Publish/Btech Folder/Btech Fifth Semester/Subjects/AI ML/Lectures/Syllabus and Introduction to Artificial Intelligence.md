### Syllabus: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

#### Course Structure
- **Lectures:** 2 hours
- **Labs:** 3 hours

#### Internal Continuous Assessment (ICA) Breakdown

|Activity|Marks|
|---|---|
|M1 + M2|10 each|
|Lab Submissions|10|
|Assignments|5|
|Research Activities|5|
|Project|10|

**Research Activities:**
- Focus on practical research (e.g., dwm)
- Review multiple research papers and summarize key learnings from 3 selected papers

**Project:**
- Develop the most optimal model using multiple techniques
- Submission of research activity and project after Term Test 2

#### Units Covered in Term Test 1

- **Unit 1:**
    - Introduction to AI
    - Properties & Classification
    - Role of AI in Cybersecurity
- **Unit 2:**
    
    - Feature Engineering: Dataset Richness
- **Unit 3:**
    
    - Supervised Learning
    - Classification
    - Unsupervised Learning
    - Note: Coverage of DWM topics depends on class progress and sensitivity

#### Recommended Books

- Mark Fenner: ML for Python
- Laurent Gill and Allan Liska: Security with AI and Machine Learning (O'Reilly)
- Emmanuel Tsukerman
- Anuradha Srinivasan
