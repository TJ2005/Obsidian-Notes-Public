## **Drone Batteries and Power Considerations**
Batteries act as the **primary power source** for drones, directly influencing flight time, stability, payload capacity, and performance. The most widely used battery type in drones is the **LiPo (Lithium Polymer)** battery due to its **high energy density** and **lightweight structure**.

---

### **Example: Typical LiPo Battery**

A **2200 mAh 3S (11.1V)** LiPo battery weighs approximately **200 g** and stores about **24.42 watt-hours (Wh)** of energy.
This can be calculated as:

$$ E = V \times \frac{mAh}{1000} $$
$$ E = 11.1 \times 2.2 = 24.42 , Wh $$

Such batteries provide sufficient energy for compact drones, allowing **longer flight durations** without significantly increasing weight.

---

### **Key Features of Drone Batteries**

1. **High Energy Density:**

   * More energy stored per gram → longer flight times.
2. **High Discharge Rate (C-Rating):**

   * Determines how quickly current can be drawn without damage.
   * Example: a 2200 mAh 25C battery can safely deliver
     $$ 2.2 \times 25 = 55 , A $$
3. **Low Weight:**

   * Critical for maintaining efficient thrust-to-weight ratio.
4. **Rechargeable and Replaceable:**

   * Allows extended flight operations by swapping batteries.

---

### **Types of Batteries Used in Drones**

| Type                            | Description             | Advantages                       | Limitations               |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| **Lithium Polymer (LiPo)**      | Standard in most drones | Lightweight, high discharge rate | Requires careful charging |
| **Lithium-Ion (Li-ion)**        | Cylindrical cells       | Long lifespan, stable voltage    | Heavier than LiPo         |
| **Nickel-Cadmium (NiCd)**       | Older tech              | Durable                          | Heavy, low energy density |
| **Nickel-Metal Hydride (NiMH)** | Moderate capacity       | Safer than LiPo                  | Less efficient, heavier   |

---

### **Battery Performance Impact**

* **Higher capacity (mAh)** → longer flight time.
* **Higher voltage (S count)** → more motor RPM ($\text{RPM} = KV \times V$).
* **Higher discharge rate (C)** → supports high-thrust maneuvers.
* **Excessive weight** → reduces efficiency and endurance.

---

### **Battery Safety and Maintenance**

* Avoid overcharging or deep discharge (keep between 20–80%).
* Store at **3.8V per cell** for longevity.
* Use **LiPo-safe chargers** and **fireproof storage**.
