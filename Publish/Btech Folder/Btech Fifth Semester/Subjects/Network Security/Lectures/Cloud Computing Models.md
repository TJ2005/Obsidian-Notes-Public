### Cloud Risks
- Abuse of service
	- Nefarious cloud computing, Botnet
	- **Counter Measures**
		- Strict Registration
		- Monitor for Credit Fraud
		- Inspection of Traffic
		- Blacklist/Whitelist
		- Other Policies
- Insecure Interface or API's
	- Miscalculation of User Resources, Letting people access other peoples resources, Cross Contamination of Servers
	- **Counter Measures**
		- Analyze Security models
		- Strong AUTH
		- Dependency chain 
- Malicious Insiders
	- Sabotaging services, Stealing Data, Attackers Know exactly what they are doing.
	- **Counter Measures**
		- Careful Management
		- Legal Contracts for Human Resources
		- Transparency
- Shared Technology Issues
	- Imagine an IOT Company that manages the thermostat of every room. now if even one Service Consumer violates the policies and makes the system vulnerable they will kill the entire customer base of the company because they have Shared Compute Resources.
	- **Counter Measures**
		- Minimize Weaknesses in Tenants
		- Monitor the environment
- Data Loss and Leakage
	- . If someone breaks into that vault or if the vault accidentally drops its contents, the result is data loss or leakage, which can be one of the most devastating outcomes of a security breach.
	- **Counter Measures**
		- Strong API Access Control
		- Encryption
		- Assess Data Protection
		- Key Safety
	- Account or service hijacking
		- Imagine you give someone the keys to your house, but instead of a trusted friend, it turns out to be a thief.  this happens when attackers steal account credentials—like usernames, passwords, or API keys
		- **Counter Measures**
			- Never share account credentials
			- two-factor authentication (2FA)
			- Actively monitor accounts


### Data Protection in Cloud


- Governance
	- Extend organizational practices about the policies, procedures, and standards used for application development and service provisioning in the cloud, and the design, implementation, testing, use, and monitoring of deployed or engaged services.
- Compliance
	- various types of laws and regulations
	- Review and assess the cloud provider’s offerings to ensure that the organizational requirements are met and the contract terms adequately meet the requirements.
- Trust
	- Complete Security somewhere always relies on trust.
- Architecture
	- Blueprints
- IAM
	- Manage who can access and who can't
- Software Isolation
	- Understand virtualization and other logical isolation techniques
- Data protection
	- to secure data while at rest, in transit, and in use, and to sanitize data
- Incident response
