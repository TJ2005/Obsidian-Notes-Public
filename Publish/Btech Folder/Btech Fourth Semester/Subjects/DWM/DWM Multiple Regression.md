### Introduction:
Multiple linear regression (MLR) is a statistical technique that uses several explanatory variables to predict the outcome of a response variable. It is an extension of ordinary least-squares regression.

### Applications:
1. Marketing: To predict sales.
2. Healthcare: To estimate patient outcomes.
3. Economics: To forecast economic indicators.
4. Education: To analyze student performance.

### Advantages:
1. Determines relative influence of variables.
2. Identifies outliers and anomalies.

### Disadvantages:
1. Pitfalls of incomplete data.
2. Incorrect conclusions due to outliers.
