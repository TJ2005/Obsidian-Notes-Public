# Fermat’s little theorem, Euler’s theorem
## Fermat
$A^{p-1} \equiv 1~ \text{mod}(p)$ 
$A^{p}\equiv A~\text{mod}(p)$


## Question
$$3^{12}\text{mod}11$$

$$= 3^{11}\times 3$$
$$=3^{11}|11|\times 3 \text{mod}(11)$$
$$ = 9$$
## Eulers Theorem
### First Version
if $A$ and $n$ are CoPrime then 
$$
A^{\phi(n)}=1~~\text{mod}(n)
$$
### Version Two
If $N=p \times q$  , $a<n$ and $k$ is an integer then
$$A^{k \phi(n)+1}\equiv a ~~\text{mod}(n)$$
