# Morning Name: Houndstooth Check

The houndstooth check, often simply referred to as houndstooth, is a distinctive textile pattern that has captured the imagination of fashion enthusiasts and designers alike. This note delves into the characteristics, history, and variations of the houndstooth pattern.

## Characteristics of Houndstooth

Houndstooth is a duotone pattern characterized by broken checks or abstract four-pointed shapes. While it is most commonly seen in black and white, the pattern can be rendered in a variety of colors, making it versatile for different styles and occasions.

![|250](https://i0.wp.com/arnoldzwicky.s3.amazonaws.com/HoundstoothPattern.jpg)

### Key Features:

- **Pattern Type**: Broken checks or abstract shapes.
- **Color Scheme**: Traditionally black and white, but available in various color combinations.
- **Structure**: Typically woven in a simple 2:2 twill, where two threads go over and two threads go under in both the warp and weft.

## Historical Background

The origins of the houndstooth pattern can be traced back to the woven tweed cloth of the Scottish Lowlands. Over time, it has transcended its traditional roots and is now utilized in a wide range of fabrics beyond wool.

### Notable Historical Points:

- **Early Use**: The pattern gained prominence in the fashion world when De Pinna, a high-end clothier based in New York City, included houndstooth checks in its 1933 spring collection of men’s suits.
- **Terminology**: The term "houndstooth" itself was not recorded until 1936, indicating its relatively recent entry into the fashion lexicon.

## Comparison with Other Patterns

Houndstooth is often compared to other classic patterns, such as herringbone and paisley. While herringbone features a V-shaped weaving pattern, houndstooth stands out with its bold, abstract checks.

### Personal Reflection:

Reflecting on personal style, one might recall a preference for herringbone tweed coats and paisley ties, yet there is an undeniable admiration for the complex boldness that houndstooth embodies.

## Variations and Modern Usage

The houndstooth pattern has evolved over the years, with many color variants emerging. For instance, a handsome brown houndstooth sport coat from Brooks Brothers exemplifies how this pattern can be adapted to modern fashion sensibilities.
