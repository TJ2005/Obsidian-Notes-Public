### **Attack & Defence Directory – MITRE**

MITRE provides structured resources for understanding **adversary behavior (attack)** and **defensive strategies**. The primary directories are **MITRE ATT\&CK** and **MITRE CALDERA**, which are widely used by cybersecurity professionals.

---

## **Main Note**

### **1. [MITRE CALDERA](https://caldera.mitre.org/)**

* **What It Is**:
  An **automated adversary emulation platform** built on the MITRE ATT\&CK framework.
* **What Information It Contains**:

  * Built-in **attack plugins** mapped to ATT\&CK techniques.
  * **Operation logs** and reports for red/blue team exercises.
* **Best Use Case**:
  ✅ **Red Team / Penetration Testing Simulation**
  ✅ **Blue Team Defense Testing** – validate if your detection rules can catch simulated attacks.

---

### **2. [MITRE ATT\&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/)** 
Introduced in Lab on 22-07-2025  10:49
* **What It Is**:
  A **global knowledge base** of real-world adversary tactics and techniques.
* **What Information It Contains**:

  * **Tactics & Techniques Matrix** (Initial Access, Privilege Escalation, etc.).
  * **Threat Group Profiles** (APT groups, malware families).
  * **Detection & Mitigation guidance**.
* **Best Use Case**:
  ✅ **Threat Hunting & Detection Engineering**
  ✅ **Mapping Attack Surface** for penetration testing
  ✅ **Learning how real attackers behave**

---

### 3. [NVD CVE Database – CVE-2021-30860](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/cve-2021-30860)

* **What It Is**:
  The **National Vulnerability Database** entry for a specific vulnerability (FORCEDENTRY – used by Pegasus).
* **What Information It Contains**:

  * **CVSS Score** (severity rating)
  * **Affected Products & Versions**
  * **Technical description of the vulnerability**
  * **References to patches or advisories**
* **Best Use Case**:
  ✅ **Vulnerability Assessment** – check if your systems are exposed
  ✅ **Exploit Research** – learn how real zero-clicks work
  ✅ **Patch Management Planning**
