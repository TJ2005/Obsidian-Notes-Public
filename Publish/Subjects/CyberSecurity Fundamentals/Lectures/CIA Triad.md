- Something that can harm u given an opportunity
- Malicious Malignant
- Malignant Threats are always present
- Threats - Example
	- Viruses, Trojan horses, etc
	- Denial of service
	- Stolen Customer Data
	- Modified Database
	- Identify Theft and other threats to personal privacy
	- Equipment Theft
	- Espionage in cyberspace
	- CyberTerrorism

- Typees of threat
	- Interception - C
	- Interruption - A
	- Modification - I
	- Fabrication - I

- Opportunity - Vulnerability

		- Imposing too many password leads to putting strain
