# Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

### Introduction to AI

- **Definition:** Coined by John McCarthy (1927 - 2011), AI involves making computers act like human beings.
- **Concepts:**
    - Basic conditional statements (if-else) can be considered a form of AI.
    - Hallucination in AI refers to generating incorrect or misleading information.
    - Principle: Human intelligence can be defined in a way that a machine can mimic the human mind.
    - Applications in various fields including health.
