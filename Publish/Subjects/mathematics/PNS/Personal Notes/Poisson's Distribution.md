# Poisson Distribution
This distribution is a limiting case of binomial distribution. Under the following assumptions.
- The number of trials $n$ tends to $\infty$.
- The probability of trial $p$ tends to $0$
- $\lambda=n\times p=finite$
Random Variable follows Poisson's distribution with parameter $\lambda$ if it assumes the non negative integral values and its probability distribution is given by 
$$ 
P(X=x) = \frac{e^{-\lambda}\lambda^x}{x!}
$$
#### Note
$X~P(\lambda)$
The expected / average / mean of the Poissons distribution is lambda
