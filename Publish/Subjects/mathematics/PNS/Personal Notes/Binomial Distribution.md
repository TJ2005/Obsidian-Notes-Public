# Binomial Distribution
Imagine you are asked to calculate the probability of getting a heads 50 Times out of a coin that is a tossed 90 Times.
In such case to calculate the probability of this event we can formulate a basic formulae and we will try to get a basic all inclusive applicable formulae.
### Actual Definition
IF an experiment has two outcomes ( Success & Failure ) is repeated $n$ times such an experiment is categorized as binomial distribution.

Suppose the random variable $X$ Denotes the number of **successes** in $n$ trials and let $P$ be the probability of success in each trial
$X - B(n,p)$


$$
P(X=x)=C_{r}^np^r q^{n-r}
$$
#### Note  Z
- Mean of the binomial distribution is $n\times p$
- Variance of binomial distribution is $n \times p \times (1-p)$
