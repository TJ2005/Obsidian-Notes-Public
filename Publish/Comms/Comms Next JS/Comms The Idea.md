# Comms//

## **What is Comms?**

Comms is a lightweight and hassle-free platform designed to facilitate communication and data sharing.
- **How it Works**:
    - Users generate a unique room code.
    - Others can join using the same code.
    - The room enables sharing of text blobs and simple messages.
No accounts, logins, or extra steps—just effortless interaction.

---

## **Why Create Comms?**

Existing platforms make basic data sharing unnecessarily cumbersome, especially for students and non-technical users.
- **Pain Points Addressed**:
    - Link bans and network firewalls hinder easy sharing.
    - Overcomplicated platforms like Teams often frustrate users.

Comms offers a streamlined alternative that is quick, simple, and effective.

---

## **Who is Comms For?**

Comms is tailored primarily for **students in colleges**:
- Aimed at those who frequently need to exchange data during group projects, classroom interactions, or casual collaborations.
- Ideal for situations where platforms like Teams are inconvenient or impractical.
